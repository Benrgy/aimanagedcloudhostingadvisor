When to Choose Linode Instead

DigitalOcean is my default recommendation, but Linode makes sense in specific situations:

→ Your target audience is in specific regions
Linode has data centers in Mumbai, Tokyo, Singapore, Sydney, Toronto, and others. If your audience is concentrated in a region where Linode has coverage but DigitalOcean does not, Linode may give better latency.
→ You have existing Linode/Akamai infrastructure
If your team already uses Linode for other services, there can be advantages to keeping your stack on one provider for billing and access management.
→ Price difference is negligible for your plan
At the 1GB level, both providers cost $12/month — identical. The Linode cost premium only becomes meaningful at 2GB and above.

My Recommendation for Small Sites

✅ Start with DigitalOcean 2GB at $22/month

It handles 1-3 WordPress sites comfortably, has the best performance in my testing, costs less than Linode at the same spec, and has the largest community of Cloudways users (meaning more tutorials and faster support familiarity). Frequently Asked Questions

Should I choose DigitalOcean or Linode on Cloudways?

For most small sites and blogs, DigitalOcean is the better choice on Cloudways: lower starting price ($12/mo vs $12/mo, same), faster server provisioning, larger global presence, and better overall performance in EU/US regions. Linode (now Akamai Cloud) is a solid alternative if you need specific data center locations not covered by DigitalOcean.

Is DigitalOcean faster than Linode on Cloudways?

In my testing from Amsterdam, DigitalOcean AMS3 averaged 0.19s TTFB vs Linode EU-West at 0.22s TTFB — a small but measurable difference. Both are dramatically faster than shared hosting. The gap narrows significantly when Cloudflare CDN is enabled on both.

Can I switch cloud providers on Cloudways?

Yes, but not directly. You need to create a new server on the different provider, migrate your applications to it, then delete the old server. Cloudways has a built-in clone feature that makes this straightforward. Budget 30-60 minutes for the switch.

Which Cloudways plan is best for a small blog?

For a small blog with under 20,000 monthly visitors, the DigitalOcean 1GB plan at $12/month is sufficient. Once you exceed 20,000 visitors/month or add WooCommerce, upgrade to the 2GB plan at $22/month.
