Step-by-Step: How to Start Your Trial

1
Go to Cloudways and click 'Start Free'
Visit cloudways.com and click the free trial button. You will be asked for your name, email address, and a password. No payment information required at this step.
Tip: Use your real name and business email. Cloudways support is excellent and knowing your name helps build the relationship.
2
Choose your cloud provider and server size
Select DigitalOcean as your cloud provider and 1GB or 2GB RAM as your server size. For the trial, 1GB is fine for testing. If you plan to convert to a paid plan, choose the size you actually want.
DigitalOcean is recommended for most users: best price-to-performance ratio and fastest provisioning (under 3 minutes).
3
Install WordPress
In the Cloudways dashboard, click 'Add Application', select WordPress, give it a name, and click install. Your WordPress site is ready in about 2 minutes.
4
Migrate your existing site (optional)
Install the Cloudways WordPress Migrator plugin on your current site. Enter your Cloudways SFTP credentials and let it migrate your files and database automatically.
Migration time depends on site size: small blogs migrate in 5-10 minutes, larger sites with many images in 20-45 minutes.
5
Run your speed tests
Use GTmetrix (free) and Google PageSpeed Insights to test your site speed on Cloudways. Compare against your current host to see the improvement.
Enable Breaker cache and Redis before testing for best results. Both are toggles in the Application Settings panel.
6
Decide: convert or cancel
If Cloudways impressed you, add your payment method before the 3 days end. If not, simply do not add payment — your server will be suspended automatically with no charge.

How to Get the Most From 3 Days

Use this checklist to evaluate Cloudways properly in your trial period:

Migrate your real site (not a test)
A real migration gives you accurate performance data. Use the Cloudways Migrator plugin — it takes under 30 minutes.
Run GTmetrix before and after
Test your site on your current host first. Then run the same test on Cloudways. The comparison makes the value immediately obvious.
Enable Breaker cache and Redis
These are not enabled by default. Breaker is in Application Settings → Caching. Redis is in Application Settings → Packages. Enable both before testing.
Test live chat support
Ask a real question. Gauge response time and quality. Good hosting support is worth paying for — Cloudways' is consistently under 10 minutes.
Check the monitoring dashboard
Look at CPU and RAM usage under your real load. This helps you choose the right server size for your paid plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a credit card for the Cloudways free trial?

No. Cloudways does not require a credit card to start the 3-day free trial. You only need to provide payment information when you want to continue after the trial ends.

What happens after the Cloudways 3-day free trial?

After 3 days, your server is suspended unless you add a payment method. Your data is retained for a short period after suspension. You can reactivate by adding a credit card or PayPal. If you do not reactivate, the server and its data are eventually deleted.

Can I use Cloudways free trial for production?

Technically yes, but not recommended. The trial server is a real cloud server with full performance. However, since it can be suspended after 3 days, do not point a live production domain to it until you have added payment information.

What is included in the Cloudways free trial?

The free trial includes a fully functional cloud server on DigitalOcean, WordPress installation, Breaker cache, Redis, free SSL, automated backups, and access to 24/7 live chat support. All features are included — it is not a limited demo.

How do I get the most out of the Cloudways free trial?

Use the 3 days to: install WordPress and migrate your existing site using the Cloudways Migrator plugin, run speed tests comparing your old host to Cloudways, test the support quality by asking a question via live chat, and check your PageSpeed score before and after enabling Breaker cache and Redis.
